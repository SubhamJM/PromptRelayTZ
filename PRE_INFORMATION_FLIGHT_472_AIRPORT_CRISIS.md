## 3. Governing Operational Directives & Safety Protocols

All airport dispatch commands and incident responses are governed strictly by the following three statutory safety regulations:

* **Rule SEC-101 (Sterile Zone Unattended Baggage Protocol):**  
  *Mandate:* Any unattended luggage or unverified baggage detected in passenger boarding concourses, gate waiting lounges, or sterile departure zones must be treated as a potential explosive hazard. Security personnel must immediately cordon off the perimeter and dispatch a certified K9 Explosive Detection Unit to conduct a non-destructive sweep.

* **Rule OPS-204 (Pre-Departure Security Hold Protocol):**  
  *Mandate:* No commercial passenger aircraft assigned to an active boarding gate may be cleared for pushback, engine start, or departure while an unresolved security anomaly, unattended baggage incident, or sterile zone breach remains under investigation at that gate. Passenger boarding must be immediately suspended.

* **Rule CLR-305 (Operational Resumption & Sterile Clearance Protocol):**  
  *Mandate:* Normal gate operations, boarding continuation, and departure clearance may only resume once:
  1. The K9 Explosive Detection Unit certifies the suspicious baggage non-hazardous;
  2. Passenger ownership is verified against baggage tag manifests; and
  3. A written Sterile Zone Clearance Certificate is countersigned by the Airport Security Controller.

## 4. Tactical Investigative Relay Playbook

To ensure your team scores maximum points across prompt engineering merit (60 pts) and final case resolution (40 pts), review these operational guidelines:

### Phase 1: Directing Agent 1 (Evidence Extraction)
* **Define a Clear Operational Persona:** Instruct Agent 1 to act strictly as a *Forensic Evidence Extractor*. Establishing this persona ensures disciplined, high-density data parsing.
* **Target Specific Identifiers:** Explicitly prompt the agent to extract exact camera and log timestamps (e.g. 08:14:23, 08:47:10, 08:49), passenger and personnel names (Daniel Kim, Aarav Mehta, Officer R. Nair), gate designations (Gate B07, Island A), and physical artifact details (blue hard-shell suitcase, baggage tag identifiers).
* **Distinguish Verified Records from Informal Chatter:** Direct the agent to separate hard, confirmed data (CCTV logs, FIDS records, official security memos) from unverified third-party passenger impressions.
* **Filter Ambient Clutter:** Instruct the agent to disregard irrelevant background noise (such as city marathon headlines, lounge catering logs, or unrelated Dubai flight delays) so that downstream agents are not distracted.
* **Enforce Strict Neutrality:** Emphasize that Agent 1 must *never* make premature accusations, infer root causes, or issue dispatch commands. The goal is pure, objective factual extraction.

### Phase 2: Directing Agent 2 (Timeline Reconstruction)
* **Define the Reconstructor Role:** Direct Agent 2 as a *Timeline & Incident Reconstructor* whose sole mandate is organizing extracted facts into an unbroken chronological sequence.
* **Enforce Temporal Structuring:** Instruct the agent to sequence all events explicitly by what happened **first**, what occurred **next**, and what happened **last**, anchoring every milestone with exact timestamps from initial check-in (08:14) through concourse movements and apron loading (09:02).
* **Trace Physical Object Movement:** Prompt Agent 2 to map the continuous custody and transit of the blue luggage from its drop at check-in counter A to its abandonment near the Gate B07 window seats.
* **Establish Causal Linkages:** Prompt the agent to highlight the causal connection between the unattended bag's discovery at 08:49 and the pending departure clearance of Flight SW 472.
* **Isolate Unverified Sightings:** Ensure the reconstructor keeps uncertain sightings (such as the unidentified hooded individual on Corridor B) distinct from established facts, without jumping to speculative conclusions.

### Phase 3: Directing Agent 3 (Logic & Decision Solving)
* **Define the Solver Persona:** Frame Agent 3 as a *Logic Decision Solver and Security Dispatch Authority*.
* **Rule-Based Evaluation:** Direct Agent 3 to evaluate the reconstructed chronological timeline against governing protocols—specifically citing **Rule SEC-101** and **Rule OPS-204**.
* **Authorize Explicit Operational Dispatch:** The decision must clearly order an immediate **HOLD** on Flight SW 472 at Gate B07, suspend passenger boarding, cordon the seating area, and dispatch the K9 explosive unit.
* **State Blocking and Resumption Conditions:** Ensure the agent explicitly defines what blocks clearance (unattended blue suitcase awaiting K9 sweep and baggage tag verification for passenger Daniel Kim) and what is required to resume operations.
* **Mandate Machine-Readable Output:** Demand strictly valid JSON formatting with no conversational preamble or markdown postamble, adhering to standard operational keys:
