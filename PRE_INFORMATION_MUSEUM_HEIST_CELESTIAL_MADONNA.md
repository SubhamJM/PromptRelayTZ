## 1. Executive Incident Briefing

On the night of 24 October 2024, during the Annual Autumn Benefactors Gala at the prestigious Riverton Museum of Fine Arts, an unprecedented security breach resulted in the theft of the institution's crown jewel: **The Celestial Madonna** (15th-century Renaissance oil on canvas, accession record **RM-1887-04**).

The heist was executed under the cover of a coordinated electrical outage in Wing C. Although a framed painting remains on display in Gallery 4, preliminary emergency scans reveal an alarming anomaly: the painting on the wall is an expert synthetic forgery. The original masterwork was cut from its stretcher bars, rolled, and smuggled into the museum's subterranean service corridors.

Your team operates as the **Art Crime Prompt Relay Unit**, deploying a three-agent analytical pipeline:

1. **Player 1 (Evidence Extraction):** Directs Agent 1 to extract forensic observations, optical feeds, telemetry, and physical clues from the museum's archives without making speculative leaps.
2. **Player 2 (Incident Reconstruction):** Directs Agent 2 to reconstruct the chronological sequence of movements, corridor transits, and causal dependencies linking the blackout to the physical swap.
3. **Player 3 (Logic & Decision Solver):** Directs Agent 3 to evaluate the reconstructed sequence against museum security and conservation regulations, unmasking the perpetrators and proving the theft mechanism in strict JSON.

## 3. Governing Art Crime Directives & Regulatory Protocols

The investigation and containment protocols are governed by the Riverton Museum Charter and International Art Crime Standards:

* **Rule SEC-401 (Auxiliary Power Blackout Lockdown Protocol):**  
  *Mandate:* Upon any intentional tripping or failure of primary electrical breaker circuits (specifically Breaker B-04) during active exhibition hours, all museum perimeter egress routes, freight loading docks, and subterranean service corridors must be placed under immediate automated biometric lockdown.

* **Rule ART-302 (Masterpiece Forgery & Physical Chain of Custody Standard):**  
  *Mandate:* Whenever an exhibited masterwork exhibits synthetic pigments, modern acrylic binders, or optical discrepancies indicating an in-situ replica swap, all conservation staff possessing departmental keycards and private vault access must immediately surrender access tokens and submit to administrative containment.

* **Rule CLR-505 (Art Crime Evidence Recovery & Containment Protocol):**  
  *Mandate:* To prevent illicit off-site transport of stolen cultural property, security teams must immediately execute a targeted search of secondary drop points identified in intercepted logistics (specifically Basement Locker 14) and seal corresponding private cloakroom and vault depositories (Unit C-408).
