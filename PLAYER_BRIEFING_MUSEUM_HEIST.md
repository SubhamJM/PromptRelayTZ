# OPERATION WINTER GALA: THE RIVERTON MUSEUM HEIST
### Confidential Team Field Briefing & Relay Operations Manual

---

## 1. Case Background

An evening of celebration turned into an audacious crime scene at the prestigious Riverton Museum of Art and History. During the exclusive Winter Gala Preview, attended by patrons, curators, and museum personnel, the institution’s crown jewel—the priceless Renaissance masterpiece *The Celestial Madonna*—was stolen from Wing C, Gallery 4. The theft unfolded in the shadows of an abrupt, intentional 90-second electrical blackout that plunged the gallery wing into pitch darkness.

When emergency backup power restored illumination, a canvas still hung inside the ornate frame, but conservation specialists and security staff quickly discovered that a sophisticated forgery had been substituted in place of the original masterwork. Amidst the gala crowd, staff chatter, and evening delivery commotion, someone executed an inside job right under the museum’s surveillance network.

A specialized 3-person intelligence task force has been assembled to coordinate an AI investigative relay. To crack this heist before the trail goes cold, your team must collaborate across three distinct, synchronized stages of inquiry—dissecting incoming forensic evidence, mapping the sequence of events, and delivering the decisive verdict.

---

## 2. Team Relay Operations & Field Guidance

### Turn 1: Evidence & Surveillance Extraction
The investigation begins with the first analyst, who faces a flood of raw visual material including gallery surveillance cameras, electronic console logs, and incident notebooks. Your AI operative at this desk depends on thoughtful instructions that guide it to separate genuine clues from ambient noise. It is essential to prompt your agent to carefully catalog meaningful facts—noteworthy physical objects, suspect movements, visual anomalies, timestamps, and names—while deliberately bypassing innocent background distractions such as catering deliveries, dropped crates, or casual event chatter. Establishing a clean, disciplined foundation of extracted facts prevents the rest of your team from chasing false trails.

### Turn 2: Chronological Timeline Reconstruction
Once the primary facts have been recorded, the baton passes to the second analyst to bridge the gap between isolated observations and narrative clarity. The objective here is guiding your synthesis agent to weave the scattered findings into a seamless, chronological timeline. By charting how individuals moved and what transpired before, during, and immediately after the power failure, this desk uncovers how the blackout window was exploited. The key to mastering this turn is directing your agent to focus purely on the objective sequence of events, linking actions together without rushing ahead to accuse suspects or spoil the final conclusion prematurely.

### Turn 3: Deductive Analysis & Final Verdict
In the decisive final turn, the third analyst directs the deduction agent to close the net on the conspiracy. Armed with the reconstructed timeline and operational standards, your agent must synthesize the clues to resolve the crime in full. Direct your AI in natural language to answer the four core questions of the case: identifying the true culprit behind the art swap, explaining the exact method and deception used to carry out the heist, revealing the underlying motive, and spotlighting the decisive smoking-gun clue that provides undeniable proof of guilt.

---

## 3. Team Strategy & Playbook Advice

Investigative prompting is an art of clarity and precision. Writing concise, purposeful instructions in everyday English allows your AI agents to extract and reason with maximum focus. Remember that this operation is a true relay: each agent relies entirely upon the insights uncovered by the teammate before them. Clear communication, attention to subtle details, and tight teamwork will guide your desk to the top of the leaderboard.
