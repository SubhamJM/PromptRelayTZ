# OPERATION CLEAR SKIES: SKYWAYS TERMINAL 1 CRISIS
### Confidential Team Field Briefing & Relay Operations Manual

---

## 1. Case Background

A high-stakes situation is unfolding across Terminal 1 at SkyWays International Airport during the height of the morning departure rush. As passengers gathered for Flight SW 472 bound for New York, a high-value cargo shipment consisting of $3.5 million in industrial diamonds was scheduled for pre-departure loading onto the aircraft. Minutes before general boarding commenced, an unattended blue hard-shell suitcase was discovered resting beneath window seats near Gate B07, immediately triggering security alarms across the sterile passenger concourse.

While standard first responders moved to establish an observation perimeter around the unattended bag, security intelligence quickly suspected a far more coordinated and deceptive scheme. The abandoned suitcase appears to be a calculated distraction—a decoy planted in the departure lounge to draw security focus, freeze gate operations, and create chaos, masking a covert baggage swap and high-value cargo theft taking place on the tarmac before the aircraft pushes back.

A specialized 3-person airport security intelligence task force has been deployed to coordinate an AI investigative relay. Across three interconnected stations, your team will guide AI agents to analyze visual forensic telemetry, trace passenger and baggage movements, and apprehend the perpetrator before the stolen diamonds vanish beyond the perimeter.

---

## 2. Team Relay Operations & Field Guidance

### Turn 1: Evidence & Surveillance Extraction
The investigation begins with the first operator at the front-line surveillance desk, reviewing multi-camera CCTV feeds, check-in scale telemetry, and terminal monitors. The primary challenge is guiding your extraction agent to spot and record critical signals buried within a bustling commercial airport. Direct your AI to lock onto key visual details—baggage descriptions, scale readings, uniform identifiers, timestamps, and suspect movements—while filtering out everyday traveler chatter, unrelated flight delays, and irrelevant morning newspaper stories. Equipping your team with an accurate, uncluttered factual foundation is essential for uncovering the underlying operation.

### Turn 2: Chronological Timeline Reconstruction
Taking the baton from the front line, the second operator oversees the tactical tracking desk. Here, your agent’s task is assembling the isolated data points into an unbroken, chronological timeline linking check-in activities, concourse movements, and apron loading operations. Instruct your agent to trace the exact chain of events leading up to the gate incident, mapping how individuals moved, when key transactions occurred, and how the distraction was orchestrated, all while maintaining strict analytical discipline and avoiding premature accusations or spoiler conclusions.

### Turn 3: Incident Resolution & Final Verdict
In the decisive final phase, the third operator commands the security decision desk. Taking the reconstructed chronological chain of events and operational facts, your agent must deduce the final verdict with absolute clarity. Instruct your AI in natural language to resolve the case by answering the four core questions: pinpointing the true culprit behind the conspiracy, explaining the precise mechanism and method used for the luggage swap, revealing the underlying motive, and highlighting the decisive physical clue or audit record that catches the perpetrator red-handed.

---

## 3. Team Strategy & Playbook Advice

Investigative prompting succeeds when instructions are direct, focused, and free of clutter. Crafting concise prompts in natural language helps your AI agents think sharply and avoid confusion. Because this mission functions as an interdependent relay, each specialist depends directly on the quality of intelligence passed down from the prior turn. Thoughtful collaboration, keen observation, and concise prompt engineering will steer your team to victory.
